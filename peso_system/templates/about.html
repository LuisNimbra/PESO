About PESO CSJDM

Public Employment Service Office, City of San Jose del Monte, Bulacan

How It Works

For jobseekers, in three steps.

1. Register Online

Create your profile from home or an internet cafe. No need to travel to the PESO office just to sign up.

2. Get Matched

Our system analyzes your skills, education, and experience to recommend the job categories that fit you best.

3. Get Your Referral

Apply to a matching vacancy and your referral slip is generated instantly. Bring it directly to the employer to start the hiring process.

For Jobseekers

Looking for work in CSJDM?

  • Register and build your profile
  • See which job categories fit you best
  • Browse active vacancies
  • Apply and track your referrals online
For Employers

Hiring workers in CSJDM?

  • Register your company with PESO
  • Post job vacancies directly
  • Receive applicants matched to your vacancies
